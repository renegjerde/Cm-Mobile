# Changelog – CM Mobile v3.72

## AI transfer market and squad evolution

- AI clubs can now recruit one suitable free agent per club per season when their senior squad has a genuine headcount or positional shortage.
- Recruitment is limited by the club's strength band, player age, position need and wage level; the AI does not simply take the highest-rated free agent available.
- AI free-agent signings retain the player's career identity, receive a normal contract, and are recorded in transfer history.
- A season/club guard prevents repeated free-agent signings by the same AI club in the same season.
- EFL world-squad changes are mirrored back into the EFL market database after AI transfers and free-agent signings, reducing stale duplicate registrations.
- Contract expiry now covers the manager's squad, AI world squads, EFL database registrations and persistent European transfer squads.
- Expired players are moved into the free-agent pool once per career identity. Active loan registrations are preserved.
- AI free-agent signing audit history is capped at 1,000 records to keep save data bounded.

## Data and save compatibility

- No SQL or Supabase schema changes.
- No mass player rebalance, player identity replacement or forced squad rebuild.
- Existing transfer history is retained; new AI free-agent transactions use a distinct `ai-free-agent` event type.
- Existing player attributes and CA/PA values are not regenerated by this release.
- Do not rerun `supabase_cm_mobile.sql`.

## Validation

- All three inline JavaScript blocks pass `node --check`.
- Targeted Node tests verify contract expiry across manager, Premier League, EFL and European registries; duplicate EFL registrations; loan preservation; one-time free-agent transfer; positional fit; EFL mirror synchronization; and same-season duplicate prevention.
- The v3.71 world-development cohort harness was rerun against the v3.72 source; its 3,000-player, ten-season progression cohorts and 5,000 academy prospects still pass all reported invariants.
- ZIP CRC integrity is checked before release.

## Test limitations

- Tests use the actual extracted application functions with controlled synthetic state. They are not a full browser test, a complete 20-season transfer-market simulation, or a Supabase save/load round-trip.
- AI recruitment is deliberately conservative: at most one free-agent signing per club per season, only when the squad has a vacancy/position shortage. This release does not add AI contract negotiations, agent demands, player bidding wars or transfer-fee negotiations.
- The existing AI inter-club transfer model remains in place; v3.72 improves free-agent recruitment and registration cleanup rather than replacing the whole market simulation.
