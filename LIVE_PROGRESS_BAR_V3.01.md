# CM Mobile v3.01 — Live match progress bar

- Fixed the live match progress bar to use 90 minutes as the regulation-time denominator, including cup matches.
- At 88 minutes, the bar now shows approximately 97.8% rather than 73.3% for cup matches.
- At 90 minutes it reaches 100%; during extra time it stays full while the match clock continues.
- Halftime at 45 minutes remains at 50%.
