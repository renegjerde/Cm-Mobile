CM Mobile 4.5.0 – Spain domestic league expansion

Added a separate Spain career path alongside England. New-career selection is now Country → Division → Club.

Spain 2026/27 structure:
- LaLiga EA SPORTS: 20 clubs, 38 rounds
- LaLiga HYPERMOTION: 22 clubs, 42 rounds
- Primera Federación Group 1: 20 clubs, 38 rounds
- Primera Federación Group 2: 20 clubs, 38 rounds
- Copa del Rey: 116 entrants (112 in the first round; four Supercopa clubs enter at the Round of 32 stage)
- Supercopa de España: four clubs, semi-finals and final

Includes independent Spanish tables and schedules, AI match simulation for the other Spanish divisions, Spain-specific league table zones, promotion/relegation and the Spanish cup competitions. The 4.4.4 UEFA duplicate-participant repair is preserved.

Squad data note:
The build contains 618 distinct player names with source-based 2026/27 club assignments across the Spanish clubs. It fills 2,050 first-team roster places in total; the remaining 1,432 places are generated fictional reserves and are explicitly marked as such in player data. Coverage is uneven: some clubs have no source-seeded player names and the maximum per club is 19. This is a partial real-player first pass, not complete verified 25-player real squads for all 82 clubs. Player ages are modelled when no verified date of birth is present.

Validation scope:
JavaScript syntax, 20/22-team double round-robin schedules (all home/away pairings exactly once), 116 unique Copa del Rey entrants and stage composition, four Supercopa entrants, 25-player roster capacity without duplicate names, and three synthetic promotion/relegation season transitions with 82 unique clubs. Not tested against live Supabase, another device, or physical iPhone Safari.
