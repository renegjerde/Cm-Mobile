CM Mobile 4.2.0 – European Competition Repair

Base: CM Mobile 4.1.2 (includes iOS fixed top navigation).

Changed file:
- index.html

Repairs:
1. European knockout scheduling now catches up overdue legs and stages instead of requiring an exact week match.
2. Catch-up is sequential and bounded; a manager-controlled European fixture pauses only that competition until played.
3. Repeated processing of an already-processed domestic week still retries overdue European knockout fixtures.
4. Season transition now checks all three league-phase European competitions (Champions League, Europa League, Conference League), including completed league phase, completed knockout history, and a recorded final winner.
5. If any European competition remains incomplete, season transition is blocked before season data is reset, and the missing competition/stage is identified.
6. Full European competition data (league table, match results, knockout ties/legs, winners) is archived in appState.europeanSeasonHistory before the active season data is cleared. Up to 30 seasons are retained.

Important limitation:
- The previous 2026/27 competition data cannot be reconstructed if it was already cleared when the career advanced to 2027/28. This patch prevents recurrence and archives future completed seasons; it does not invent missing historical results.

Validation performed:

- Actual gameplay/season simulation still requires in-game testing.
