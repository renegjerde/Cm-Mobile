# CM Mobile v3.52 — junior academy graduation rule

- Academy players age 18 who have completed a season are presented with a decision at season transition: promote to senior squad or release on a free transfer.
- User decision is processed only after division membership validation succeeds.
- Promoted players are added to both the manager's squad and the world squad record.
- Players not promoted are released to free agency.
- Other academy cohorts continue to age and auto-release players once they exceed 18.

Validation: embedded JavaScript blocks pass Node syntax checks. This is not a full interactive browser test.
