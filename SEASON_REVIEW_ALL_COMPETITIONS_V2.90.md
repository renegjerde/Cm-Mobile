# CM Mobile v2.90 – Full season review

## Season overview
- Use the managed club's actual active division in the season heading and league table.
- Show performance in the domestic league, FA Cup, Carabao Cup and each European competition: status, progress, match record and goals for/against where match data exists.
- Identify the team's top scorer, assist leader and appearance leader.
- Include a squad statistics table: appearances, minutes, goals, assists, chances created, yellow cards and red cards.

## European qualification
- Calculate the five Champions League, three Europa League and one Conference League slots using the current Premier League order and the completed FA Cup / Carabao Cup winners.
- Clubs already allocated to a higher competition are not duplicated in a lower one. Cup winners affect the distribution.
- When a non-Premier-League career is active and no live Premier League table is available, the screen labels the qualification output as an estimate rather than a final table-derived allocation.

## EFL promotion review
- Display automatic promotion positions and all playoff places for Championship, League One and League Two before the manager starts the new season.
- Playoff candidates are explicitly labelled as candidates; the game resolves winners during season transition.

## Validation notes
- JavaScript syntax checked after editing.
- Qualification allocation, status rendering and promotion table helpers receive focused logic checks.
- Full interactive browser QA is not claimed unless actually completed in the release environment.
